3. Denroot — Application Disclaimer

3. Denrootアプリに関する免責事項

Not a medical or therapeutic product. Denroot is a behavioral design application intended to support personal productivity and attention management. It is not a medical device, therapeutic intervention, or treatment for any mental health condition, including attention deficit disorders. Users experiencing clinical symptoms of ADHD, anxiety, or addiction should consult a qualified healthcare professional.

On "addiction." References to "addiction" or "behavioral addiction" on this site are used in a colloquial and behavioral-science sense. "Smartphone addiction" and "social media addiction" are not formal clinical diagnoses (DSM-5 / ICD-11); gambling disorder is the recognized behavioral addiction. Comparisons to gambling refer to shared reward-system and variable-reinforcement mechanisms, not clinical equivalence.

Individual results vary. The effectiveness of behavioral design approaches differs significantly between individuals. No specific outcome is guaranteed. Claims about the behavioral effects of waiting protocols, task structures, and feedback systems are based on established behavioral science principles, not on clinical trials of Denroot specifically.

Features and pricing subject to change. All product features, pricing estimates, and timelines described on this site are indicative and subject to change without notice prior to launch.

Pre-launch status. Denroot is currently in pre-launch development. The features described represent the intended product design and may differ from the final released application.
